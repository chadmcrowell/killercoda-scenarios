# Deployment Rollout Failures - Pods Refusing to Become Ready

A developer just pushed a new image version and the deployment rollout appears to have stalled. Half the pods are running the old version and half are stuck in a not-ready state. The application is still serving traffic from old pods but the team needs you to either fix the rollout or safely roll it back.
