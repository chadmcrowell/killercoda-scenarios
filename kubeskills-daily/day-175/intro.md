# Audit Logging - Fixing Blind Spots in Your Audit Policy

Your security team has requested evidence of all secret read operations from the past 24 hours as part of a compliance review. When you check the audit log file you find it is empty despite significant cluster activity. You need to identify why the audit policy is failing to capture events and correct the configuration so that critical operations are properly recorded going forward.
